Terms of Service

1. What it is

A hobbyist trading-game engine, free on Google Play, with every feature available to everyone — including importing your own dataset (a ZIP of CSVs and images).

2. No copyrighted content

We ship none: no real player names, clubs, crests, photos or databases, and no licence for any of them. The built-in dataset is procedurally generated fiction, and the game is not affiliated with any football body, league, club or player.

3. Your data is your responsibility

Imported datasets stay on your device; the game never uploads or shares them. What you import, use or share — and having the rights to do so — is on you.

4. Licence

Personal, non-commercial use. Don't reverse-engineer the game to bypass its paid unlocks, and don't sell it or pass it off as your own.

5. Purchases

Optional paid unlocks (the Android app's “Full Upgrades”) are never required: everything they grant can also be earned by playing. Prestige Points (PP) are progression only — no cash value, no transfer, no randomness, no wagering. Google Play handles payment and refunds, under its own policy and without limiting your statutory rights; unlocks are tied to that store account, and the store can restore non-consumable purchases. The one optional rewarded ad (WATCH AD in the Agency, 1 PP, once per 30 minutes) is never required either.

6. As-is

Provided without warranty of any kind, and to the extent the law allows, we're not liable for indirect or consequential damages from using it.

7. Changes & contact

Material changes show in the date at the top of this page; continued use means you accept them. Reach us through the game's Google Play listing.

Privacy Policy

1. On your device only

No server backend, and nothing is sent anywhere by the game.

StorageWhat we store
localStorage rsd_disclaimer_accepted_v4, rsd_tutorial_seen_v3, rsd_lang, rsd_active_dataset, rsd_prestige_v1 (Agency balance, upgrades, stats, ad cooldown), retro_soccer_highscores_…, the auto-saved game, and two housekeeping flags.
IndexedDB Datasets you import (CSVs + images) and a cache of default.zip.

2. What it fetches

default.zip, the built-in fictional dataset, on load; imported ZIPs come from your own device. The only other request is an ad, and only when you tap it.

3. The rewarded ad

One ad, never a banner or interstitial, and never requested unless you tap WATCH AD. It's served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and Google may collect device and advertising identifiers (such as your Android advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP, and ad interaction data to select and measure ads. We never see it, and no game data goes with the request. Where the law requires it (EEA/UK) the SDK asks for consent first — decline and no ad is requested. Google's own policy and controls apply (policies.google.com/privacy, policies.google.com/technologies/ads); on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. What it doesn't do

No cookies, analytics, telemetry, fingerprinting, cross-site tracking or profiling, and no sending your stored data off the device. No other third-party ad or analytics code is bundled.

5. Payments

Google Play collects the money and handles post-sale support under its privacy policy. We never collect your name, email, billing address or card details.

6. Your rights

Delete everything by uninstalling the app, or use START OVER FROM ZERO in the Agency (wipes Agency progress, the saved run and the score boards; imported datasets stay until you delete them in the dataset manager). The game holds no personal data of its own, so there is no off-device record to amend, export or delete — the ad data belongs to Google, controlled as in §3.

7. Children

Not directed at under-13s and not part of Play's Families programme; no chat, social features or content visible to others.

8. Security, changes & contact

The only data is what your device stores, so its security is your device's. Material changes show in the date at the top; questions go through the game's Google Play listing.

Plain-language summary

The game keeps a few values on your device and ships no copyrighted football content; what you import is yours to clear and use. It doesn't phone home, set cookies or track you, and its one optional rewarded ad plays only when you tap it.
